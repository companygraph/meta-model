# beacon-platform

> The platform repository of beacon and, until its domain knowledge has moved into this model, the master for the domain documents under `docs/domains/` (beacon-method DSC-007 E26). Nothing syncs from it into this model: a domain fact is corrected there first and its page here follows by hand. A `source-id` from it is the `ref` in the frontmatter of a domain document, such as `DOM-11`.
