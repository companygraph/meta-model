# beacon-finance

> The finance repository of beacon. GmbH and the master for its finance principles, policies,
> posting rules, finance decisions, runbooks and compliance backlog (FE-25). Nothing syncs from it
> into this model: a finance rule, risk or control is corrected there first and its page here
> follows by hand. A `source-id` from it is a
> principle number of the finance principles (`G1` to `G13`), a decision number of the finance
> decision log (`FE-1` onward), a runbook reference (`RUN-1`), or, on a risk, the number of a gap in
> the compliance backlog, written `gap 3`.
