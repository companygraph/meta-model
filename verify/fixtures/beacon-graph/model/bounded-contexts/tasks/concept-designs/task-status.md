# Task Status

> Where a task stands in its lifecycle: `queued`, `blocked`, `claimed`, `in_progress`, `implementation_done`, `deferred`, `done`, `failed` or `cancelled`. Of these `done`, `failed` and `cancelled` are final, and `implementation_done` means the work was handed to the integrator and no one is working on it.
