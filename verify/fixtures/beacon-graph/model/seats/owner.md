# Owner

> The seat that holds the company's duty to keep books and its responsibility for them, and decides every posting, payment, approval and action toward the outside that another seat proposes.

## What it takes

A proposal with its reasoning and evidence: the account, the VAT treatment, the rule it rests on and the document it is about; for an action toward the outside, the draft of what would go out. It refuses to decide a proposal that comes without them.

## What it produces

An approval or a refusal, recorded where the proposal was made; entries released in the accounting system; payments, registrations, contracts and messages sent in the company's name.

## What it never does

- Never leaves an approval or a refusal unrecorded, so the record shows who decided what.
