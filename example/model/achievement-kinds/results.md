# Results

> What came of the work — an effect measured, a finding established or an outcome still owed.

## What it means

An achievement whose claim is the consequence rather than the act: what changed because the work was done, what it turned out to show, and what it has not delivered yet. An entry's honest unfinished business is this kind, because it is an outcome like any other.

The work that produced the result is Delivery, and the call that aimed it is Decisions. The result is filed apart from both, so a reader looking for what came of a period finds it in the same place in every entry.
