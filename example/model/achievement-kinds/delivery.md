# Delivery

> Something built, changed or taken apart, and in the hands of the people it was for.

## What it means

An achievement whose claim is the work done: a system replaced, a service split, a migration run, a process put in place. It names what changed and who has it now.

Choosing what to build is Decisions. What the change turned out to be worth — a measured effect, a finding, an outcome still owed — is Results, and it goes there even where one sentence could carry both.
