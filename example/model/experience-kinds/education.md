# Education

> A degree, a certification, a course.

## What it means

A period whose output is a qualification rather than a delivery. `organization` names the
awarding body — the school, the certifying institute — which is the one case where the field
does not name somewhere work was done.

Learning that happened during a role is not this kind. Every role teaches something; what makes
a period education is that the qualification is the point of it.
