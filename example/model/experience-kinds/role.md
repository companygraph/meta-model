# Role

> A position held in an organisation — the employment history.

## What it means

A period spent working for an organisation under an arrangement that outlasts any one piece of
work: employment, a long-running contract, a partnership. `organisation` names the employer.

It is not a delivery. What was built during a role belongs to a project, and a role that lists
one delivery in its achievements is usually two periods written as one. The test is whether the
period would have continued had the work changed: if it would, it is a role.
