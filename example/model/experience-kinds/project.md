# Project

> A delivery worth naming on its own, inside a role or beside one.

## What it means

A period defined by what it produced rather than by who it was spent with: a system, a
migration, a launch. Client work and internal work alike. `organization` names the client, or
the organization the work was done for when that differs from the employer.

It ends when the delivery does. A project that has no end and no deliverable is a role
described by its current work.
