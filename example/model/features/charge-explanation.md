# Charge explanation

> Anyone reading an invoice can tell how each charge was reached, in the words of the contract they agreed to.

## Description

Every line on an invoice carries the term of the contract that produced it and the usage that term was applied to, and the explanation beside the line is rendered from both. The person paying reads it where they read the invoice, and the finance team that issued it reads the same words when a question reaches them. It explains the arithmetic and stops there: whether the contract says what the payer believes it says is a question for the contract, and nothing here answers it.
