# Pricing rules

> A finance team can write each term of a contract as a rule every billing run applies, named as the contract names it.

## Description

A rule states what a term charges for, at what price and under which conditions, and it belongs to the contract it was written from, so a new version of the contract brings new versions of its rules. It records prices and never proposes them. A term the rules cannot express is not approximated: the term is renegotiated or the rules grow, and until one of those happens the term is not billed.
