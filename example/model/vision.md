# Billing nobody has to explain

> A customer reading one of our invoices can tell how the number was reached, without calling us and without a spreadsheet beside it.

## What it means

Every charge traces to something the customer did, in language they used when they agreed to
it. When an invoice is disputed the answer is in the invoice, and support confirms it rather
than reconstructing it. We would know this holds when disputes are resolved by reading rather
than by investigation.

It says nothing about what we charge or whom we sell to. Pricing models and market are
decisions we expect to change several times; this is what we want to be true of the bill
whatever they turn out to be.
