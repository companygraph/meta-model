# Managing Director

> The seat that leads the company and answers for its direction.

## What it takes

The state of every department, read from what each one reports rather than from how it feels, and the questions the departments cannot settle between them.

## What it produces

A direction the departments can work to, and a decision on each question put to it, with the reason written where the next reader finds it.

## What it never does

- Never does a department's work in place of its lead.
- Never leaves a question between two departments without a decision.
- Never changes a department's standard without the lead of that department hearing why.
