# Head of Product

> The seat that leads Product and answers for what is built and why.

## What it takes

What customers say they cannot do, and what the products and features already do about it.

## What it produces

A decision on what is built next and what is not, each with the customer need it answers.

## What it never does

- Never commits a feature to a date before engineering has said what it takes.
- Never decides how a feature is built; that is the engineering standard's.
- Never keeps a feature no customer need answers.
