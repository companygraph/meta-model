# Engineering Lead

> The seat that leads Engineering and sets the standard every engineer works to, wherever they sit.

## What it takes

The work of the people in Engineering and the changes the other departments' engineers ship, read against the standard the seat has set.

## What it produces

A standard for how engineering work is done and reviewed, and a department that meets it: the billing run reproducible, every charge traceable.

## What it never does

- Never lowers the standard for a change because a date is close.
- Never merges a change it has also written.
- Never leaves an engineer in another department without the standard they are held to.
