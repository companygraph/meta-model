# Verified

> The requirement is built, and a test observes what its fit criterion observes.

## What it means

A requirement is Verified when it is built and at least one test or recorded run observes what its fit criterion observes. A page enters the level when an `## Evidence` row shows `verifies`, and leaves it when that test is removed or no longer observes the fit criterion. While a requirement is Verified, a reader may rely on the product meeting it as long as the test passes.
