# Executive Assistant

> Keeps the managing director's calendar, papers and decisions in order, so that what is decided is also prepared and followed up.

## Responsibilities

- Prepares the papers for each leadership meeting and sends them before it.
- Records what the meeting decided and follows each decision up with the person it names.
