# Backend Engineer

> Builds and runs the services the billing platform stands on, and answers for them when they are not right.

## Responsibilities

- Keeps the services the product runs on correct, and takes a turn on the pager for what ships.
- Keeps each change small enough that a reviewer reads it in one sitting.
- Covers each change with the tests that show the invoice is still right.
