# Engineering Lead

> Leads Engineering and sets the standard every engineer works to, wherever they sit.

## Responsibilities

- Sets the standard for how engineering work is done and reviewed.
- Keeps Engineering meeting it: the billing run reproducible, every charge traceable.
- Keeps the standard for a change whatever the date, and gives it to every engineer in another department.
