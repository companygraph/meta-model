# Managing Director

> Leads the company and answers for its direction.

## Responsibilities

- Reads the state of every department from what each one reports.
- Sets a direction the departments can work to.
- Decides each question the departments cannot settle between them, and writes the reason where the next reader finds it.
- Leaves a department's work to its lead, and tells the lead why before changing the department's standard.
