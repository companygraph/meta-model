# Managing Director

> Leads Beacon Systems and answers for its direction, its results and the departments that carry them out.

## Responsibilities

- Sets the company's direction and says what it will not do, so the departments choose against something.
- Holds each department to the outcomes it answers for, and settles what two of them cannot.
- Signs what commits Beacon, after Legal has said what it commits the company to.
